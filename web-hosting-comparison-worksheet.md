## Questions Before Making a Decision

1. Does the hosting plan support my website's technology?
2. Are the allocated server resources sufficient?
3. What happens when my website exceeds resource limits?
4. Are renewal prices affordable?
5. Are backups and SSL included?
6. Can I upgrade without migrating my website?
7. Are there restrictions on website usage?
8. Does the provider offer suitable technical support?
